EDITION 01 · BFSI

From AI pilotsto AI-nativeinstitutions.

Banks, insurers and their GCCs are past asking whether AI works. The hard questions now are about deployment, governance, ownership and scale. The Intelligence Shift brings together the leaders answering them.

  1. STAGE 01

    AI Ambition

    Pilots and proofs of concept. Promising, contained, mostly in the lab.

  2. STAGE 02

    Enterprise Transformation

    Governance, data readiness and change at scale. Where most institutions are now.

  3. STAGE 03

    AI-Native Operations

    AI embedded in how the institution runs, every day, by default.

ON THE TABLE

Five questions.One room.
